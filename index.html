FSS + KSSN

Different functions. Shared responsibility. One goal.

Scholar + family stay at the center. FSS, KSSN and the school contribute different functions around the same identified need or barrier.

This work is broader than attendance. FSS and KSSN may work together around academics, attendance, behavior, family circumstances, integrated supports, and Community School opportunities. Start with the need, then determine the function.

KSSN ROLE CLARITY

Go. Check. Stop.

A quick decision aid for distinguishing core Community School Coordinator work from work that needs consultation or should remain with another role.

GO

Core coordinator functions.

  • Resource coordination
  • Family/community engagement
  • Community partnerships
  • Attendance team participation
  • Programs and opportunities
CHECK

Coordinate with the appropriate lead before acting.

  • Behavior interventions
  • School counseling functions
  • Supervision/lunchroom coverage
  • School-specific operational duties
STOP

Work that should remain with the appropriately assigned professional.

  • Substitute teaching
  • Ongoing therapy
  • Secretarial work
  • Serving as disciplinarian
  • Running detention
