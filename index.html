OUR OPERATING MODEL

Read the operating model

FSS: family attendance support and sustained attendance intervention. KSSN: Community School support, partners and resource coordination. Shared Work: identify the barrier, coordinate, connect supports, check impact and share patterns. School: remove school-controlled barriers and provide school-based support. Cadence: daily do the work; weekly unstick the work; monthly find patterns; quarterly change the system.

OWNERSHIP + SUPPORT

Different roles. One coordinated response.

Ownership changes with the barrier. These are functions—not separate cases.

FSS

Attendance-focused family intervention

Family voice, attendance planning, barrier-focused intervention, follow-through, and monitoring.

SHARED WORK

Coordinate when both functions add value

One barrier hypothesis, one attendance lead, distinct responsibilities, coordinated family contact, and one review point.

KSSN

Community School + integrated supports

Resource fit, partner coordination, warm connections, access, and repeated community-resource gaps.

SCHOOL

School-controlled conditions

Academic access, belonging, safety, discipline, scheduling, instructional access, and school processes.

Overlap is intentional; duplication is not. A referral does not automatically transfer ownership of the attendance goal.
